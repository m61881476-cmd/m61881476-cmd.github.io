About

Mohamed Hany Elsheikh (Arabic: محمد هاني الشيخ), also known as Mohamed Elsheikh, is an Egyptian accountant, software developer and video editor based in Cairo. He studies at the Faculty of Commerce, Capital University (formerly Helwan University), and works as a freelancer in accounting, software development and video editing.

He is best known for Daftar — Smart Accountant, a free, offline accounting app that understands Egyptian Arabic: users type or say a transaction, or photograph an invoice, and Daftar creates a balanced journal entry and updates the financial statements instantly. He also built Mozaker | Sanad, a study platform for commerce students.

Daftar — Smart Accountant (2025)

A free accounting app that works offline and understands Egyptian Arabic. It turns speech and invoice photos into balanced journal entries and produces full financial statements: income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, changes in equity and trial balance. Features include the Liquidity Compass and the Smart Notebook, and it passed an automated test of 1,200,000 sentences without a single wrong entry.
